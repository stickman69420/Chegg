The following images provide a quick summary of the move and attack sets for each minion type.
The green tiles show possible moves.
The red tiles show possible attacks.
The yellow lines show connections between attacks:
  • Rectangles show that the enclosed tiles will recieve damage at the same time
  • Tiles connected by normal yellow lines will recieve damage at the same time
  • Attacks connected by yellow arrows follow a path, and a minion in the path will block it
